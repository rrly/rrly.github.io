# SATTY — VST3 / AU / Standalone

A stereo audio saturator plugin written in C++ with JUCE. Inspired by the
Fatness/Color interaction of **Sausage Fattener** and the "always-on"
character-adding feel of saturators like **OTT**

## Features

- **6 saturation modes:** Fatness (default blended algorithm), Soft (tanh),
  Tube (asymmetric, even harmonics), Tape (rational curve, gentle),
  Diode (aggressive), Hard Clip.
- **Fatness knob:** drives the saturator from unity up to ~12x gain into the curve.
- **Color knob:** tilts the pre-saturation EQ (dark ↔ bright) and, in Fatness
  mode, crossfades between tape-style (warm, even harmonics) and tanh-style
  (clean, odd harmonics) saturation.
- **4x IIR oversampling** to keep aliasing under control when the signal is crushed.
- **DC blocker** on the output (the asymmetric modes can introduce DC).
- **Smoothed parameters** — no zipper noise when automating.
- **Dry/Wet mix**, input gain, output gain.
- **State save/restore** via `AudioProcessorValueTreeState` — presets and
  automation work out of the box.
